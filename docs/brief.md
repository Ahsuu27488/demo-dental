# Brief — demo-dental

**Client:** none. Internal portfolio piece (owner's decision, 2026-10-22): the
family shop site is not proof for international clients, so we build a demo
site for a fictional foreign small business.

**Business (fictional):** Fernwick Dental, a family dental clinic in downtown
Portland, Oregon, near Pioneer Courthouse Square. Web search on 2026-10-22
found no real clinic called "Fernwick Dental".

**Must have:** hero with offer + "Book a visit"; services with starting prices;
why us (concrete, no fake stats); how a visit works (no team); booking form
(client-side only, success state); opening hours; map of a public landmark
area; WhatsApp/call buttons with fake 555 numbers; FAQ; footer; visible demo
note on the page; basic SEO (title, description, OG image, sitemap, robots);
Lighthouse mobile 90+.

**Must not have:** testimonials, ratings, reviews, awards, insurance logos,
photos of real people as patients/staff.

**Use:** portfolio (ahsandev.site) and the Fiverr "Next.js website" gig.
Price/deadline: n/a (internal).
